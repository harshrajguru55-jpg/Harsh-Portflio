02Featured: NHS Finance

A year on a £140m budget

Finance Trainee · NHS Leicester, Leicestershire & Rutland ICB · 2024–25
The context

I joined the co-commissioning finance team supporting primary care — the budget that pays GP practices across the region. The work was live, audited, and unforgiving of errors.

What I did

Maintained transaction lists and trial balances and analysed GMS/PMS data for monthly GP payments. During year-end I processed 26+ journals — accruals, adjustments, forecasting and budget entries — and prepared audit-ready working papers. When my manager was away, I independently ran co-commissioning: 12+ forecasting papers, cash-flow analysis, and month-end reporting straight to the Senior Finance Manager.

What I took from it

How a real finance function actually runs — and that I'm comfortable owning numbers that other people depend on.
